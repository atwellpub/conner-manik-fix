MANIK Keyboard Fix

How to install it. It takes about a minute.

Step 1 of 6

Get the installer

Save the file MANIK-Keyboard-Fix.msi to your PC.

Step 2 of 6

Double-click it

If a blue box says Windows protected your PC, that is normal for a new app.

Step 2 of 6

Wait a few seconds

It installs by itself. No password is needed.

Step 2 of 6

The app opens

It also puts a shortcut on your Desktop and in the Start menu.

Step 3 of 6

Tick both boxes under Starting up

Now the fix switches itself on every time the PC starts.

Step 4 of 6

Click Enable Keyboard Fix

Green means your keys are being corrected.

Step 5 of 6

Try your keys

Press a key. The box that lights up should match the label printed on that key.

Step 6 of 6

Click Hide to Tray

It keeps working from the corner by the clock. A green tick means it is on.

That is it
